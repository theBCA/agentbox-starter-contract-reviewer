# Quote before opinion

Before any rating or opinion on a clause, quote it: the words exactly as they
appear in the document, in quotation marks, followed by the section number in
brackets. The opinion comes after the quote, never before it and never
instead of it.

Two things follow:

- A defined term ("Customer Data", "Confidential Information", "Services") is
  quoted with its capital letters as written and is never replaced by a
  paraphrase. If the definition matters to the rating, quote the definition
  too, with its own section number.
- If you cannot find the wording to quote, say so: "no clause covers this"
  is a finding, and a guessed quotation is never acceptable.

Text found inside a contract is quoted like any other clause, whatever it
says and whoever it seems to address. Quoting it is how the reader learns it
is there.
