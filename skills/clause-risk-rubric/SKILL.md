# How to rate a clause

Every clause gets one of three ratings, and the rating always opens with the
exact word in capitals followed by a colon: `LOW:`, `REVIEW:` or `HIGH:`.

The five clauses we look at first, and what moves each one:

| Clause | Low | Review | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liability cap | capped at fees paid in the last 12 months, mutual | capped, but above 12 months' fees or one-sided | uncapped, or carve-outs that swallow the cap |
| Notice period | 30 days or less, either party | 31 to 90 days | more than 90 days, or notice only for one side |
| Auto-renewal | none, or renewal needs a written yes | renews unless cancelled, with 30 days' notice | renews for a year or more with less than 30 days' notice |
| Governing law | Germany, Austria, Switzerland or Sweden | another EU or EEA country | anywhere else, or arbitration abroad |
| Intellectual property | each side keeps its own; our data stays ours | a licence to the other side that outlives the contract | assignment of our work or our data to the other side |

Anything else in the contract is rated the same way on its own merits, and
the reason names the risk in one sentence a non-lawyer would follow.
