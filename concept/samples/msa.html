between Nordlicht Outdoor GmbH ("Customer") and Cloudstack Logistics Ltd ("Supplier").

1. Services

Supplier shall provide the warehouse management platform and the related services described in each Order Form (the "Services").

Customer shall pay the Fees stated in the applicable Order Form within thirty (30) days of invoice.

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months.

3.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless terminated by written notice not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

4.1 Customer grants Supplier a perpetual, irrevocable licence to use Customer Data for the improvement of Supplier's products.
