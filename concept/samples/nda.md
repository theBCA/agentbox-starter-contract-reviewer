# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

between Nordlicht Outdoor GmbH ("Nordlicht") and Fjellvind Textiles AS
("Fjellvind"), together the "Parties".

1. Purpose. The Parties wish to exchange information to evaluate a supply
   relationship for technical outdoor fabrics (the "Purpose").

2. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any information
   disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with the Purpose that is
   marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be
   confidential.

3. Obligations. The receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only
   for the Purpose, protect it with at least the care it applies to its own
   confidential information, and disclose it only to employees and advisers
   who need to know it and are bound by obligations no less strict.

4. Exclusions. Information that is public, already known to the receiving
   Party, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party is
   not Confidential Information.

5. Term. This Agreement runs for two (2) years from signature. The obligations
   in clause 3 survive for three (3) years after the last disclosure.

6. Return. On request, the receiving Party shall return or destroy all
   Confidential Information within thirty (30) days.

7. Liability. Each Party's total liability under this Agreement shall not
   exceed EUR 100,000.

8. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Germany. The
   courts of Hamburg have exclusive jurisdiction.

9. Notices. Notices are given in writing to the addresses on the signature
   page and take effect on receipt.
