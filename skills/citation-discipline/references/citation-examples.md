# Citation examples

Without the quote, the reader has to trust the reviewer. With it, they can
check.

**Without:** The liability clause is one-sided and should be reviewed.

**With:** "Supplier's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not
exceed the Fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months." (9.1) -- REVIEW:
the cap protects the supplier only; nothing caps ours.

**Without:** The renewal terms are aggressive.

**With:** "This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods
of twelve (12) months unless terminated by written notice not less than
ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term." (3.2) -- HIGH:
a year at a time, with a ninety-day window.

**Without:** Their definition of confidential information is broad.

**With:** "'Confidential Information' means all information disclosed by
either party, whether or not marked confidential." (1.4) -- REVIEW: it
covers everything either side says, marked or not.
