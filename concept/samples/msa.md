# Master Services Agreement

between Nordlicht Outdoor GmbH ("Customer") and Cloudstack Logistics Ltd
("Supplier").

1. Services. Supplier shall provide the warehouse management platform and the
   related services described in each Order Form (the "Services").

2. Fees. Customer shall pay the Fees stated in the applicable Order Form
   within thirty (30) days of invoice.

3. Term and renewal.
   3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an
       initial term of twelve (12) months.
   3.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of
       twelve (12) months unless terminated by written notice not less than
       ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

4. Customer Data. "Customer Data" means all data uploaded to the Services by
   or on behalf of Customer.
   4.1 Customer grants Supplier a perpetual, irrevocable licence to use
       Customer Data for the improvement of Supplier's products.

5. Warranties. Supplier warrants that the Services will perform materially in
   accordance with the Documentation.

6. Liability.
   6.1 Supplier's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed
       the Fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.
   6.2 Nothing in this Agreement limits Customer's liability for any breach.

7. Termination. Supplier may suspend the Services on five (5) days' notice
   for any breach by Customer.

8. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
   Delaware. Disputes are settled by binding arbitration in Wilmington.

9. Notices. Notices are given by email to the addresses in the Order Form.
