# Worked examples

| Wording (section) | Rating and reason |
|---|---|
| "Each party's total liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim." (9.1) | LOW: mutual, and capped at a year of fees. |
| "Supplier's liability shall not exceed EUR 5,000,000." (9.1) | REVIEW: capped, but the cap is far above what we pay and it is one-sided. |
| "Nothing in this Agreement limits Customer's liability for any breach." (9.3) | HIGH: uncapped for us alone. |
| "Either party may terminate on thirty days' written notice." (12.2) | LOW: short and mutual. |
| "This Agreement renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives ninety days' notice." (3.2) | HIGH: a year at a time, and the window to leave is long and easy to miss. |
| "This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales." (17.1) | REVIEW: outside the countries we contract in by default, inside a legal system we know. |
| "Customer assigns to Supplier all right, title and interest in Customer Data." (8.4) | HIGH: our data would become theirs. |
