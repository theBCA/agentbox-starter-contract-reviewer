# Data Processing Agreement

between Nordlicht Outdoor GmbH ("Controller") and Cloudstack Logistics Ltd
("Processor"), supplementing the Master Services Agreement.

1. Subject matter. Processor processes personal data of Controller's customers
   (names, delivery addresses, order histories) solely to provide the
   Services.

2. Instructions. Processor processes personal data only on documented
   instructions from Controller.

3. Confidentiality. Persons authorised to process the data are bound by
   confidentiality.

4. Sub-processors. Processor may engage sub-processors with Controller's prior
   written consent, and remains liable for their performance.

5. Transfers. Personal data is processed within the European Economic Area.
   Any transfer outside it requires an approved transfer mechanism and
   Controller's consent.

6. Security. Processor implements appropriate technical and organisational
   measures, described in Annex 2.

7. Breach. Processor notifies Controller of a personal data breach without
   undue delay and at the latest within forty-eight (48) hours.

8. Deletion. On termination, Processor deletes or returns all personal data
   within thirty (30) days, unless retention is required by law.

9. Audit. Controller may audit Processor's compliance once a year on
   reasonable notice.
